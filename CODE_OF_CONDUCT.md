# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the ZEXX Terminal project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping others learn.
- Focusing on collaboration.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Trolling or insulting comments.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove or reject contributions that violate these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests, and community interactions.
